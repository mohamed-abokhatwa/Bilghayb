# Privacy Policy · سياسة الخصوصية

**Bil-Ghayb (بالغيب)**
Effective date: 31 October 2026

Bil-Ghayb is a free app where strangers pray (make dua) for each other anonymously. We built it to collect as little as possible. We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 1. What we collect

| Data | Required? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Anonymous account ID**, a random ID created when you open the app | Yes | To keep your needs, prayers and settings linked to your device. We never ask for your email, phone number or password. |
| **First name** | No | Shown only on needs you choose to share by name. You can always post anonymously. |
| **Sister / brother** | No | To show "a sister" or "a brother" on anonymous needs. |
| **Country** | Yes (you can choose "Elsewhere") | Shown as a flag on your needs, and to show which countries prayed for you. |
| **Language and time zone** | Yes | To show the app in your language and send reminders at the right local time. |
| **Needs you write** (dua requests, janazah requests, thank-you notes) | Only if you post | So other users can read them and pray for you. |
| **Prayers you make** (which need you prayed for, and when) | Yes, when you pray | To count prayers and avoid showing you the same person twice. |
| **Reports you send** | Only if you report | To review and remove harmful content. |
| **Push notification token** | Only if you allow notifications | To send your morning reminder and special-time reminders. |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or browsing activity.

## 2. What other users can see

- **Your needs:** the text, your first name (only if you chose to share it) or "a sister / a brother / someone", your country, the category and how many people prayed.
- **Nobody can ever see who prayed for whom.** Prayer records stay inside our database. The app shows only totals and country flags.
- Other users cannot message you. There is no chat.

## 3. Contact details are not allowed

To protect everyone, the app blocks phone numbers, emails, usernames, links, messaging apps and social media accounts in every text you write. Please do not try to share them in other ways. Needs that break this rule are removed.

## 4. How we check content

New needs are checked automatically before they are shared:
1. Rules on your device and on our server block contact details, links, money requests and abuse.
2. The text may be sent to an AI moderation service (Anthropic) to detect harmful content. Only the text of the need is sent, never your account ID, name or country. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
3. Any need reported by 3 different users is hidden until it is reviewed.

## 5. Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the app:

| Provider | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Hosts our database and anonymous sign-in. |
| **Expo (650 Industries)** | Delivers push notifications. |
| **Anthropic** | AI content moderation of need text (see section 4). |
| **Apple App Store / Google Play** | App distribution. Their own privacy policies apply. |

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

## 6. How long we keep data

- **Needs** stop being shown to new people after 60 days (janazah requests after 40 days). They stay in your "My needs" list until you delete your account.
- **Prayer records** (who prayed for which need) are deleted automatically after 90 days. Only running totals (how many duas you made and received) are kept on your profile.
- **Reports** are kept while your account exists.
- **When you delete your account** (Me → "Delete my account & data"), we permanently delete your profile, needs, prayers, reports and push token right away. This cannot be undone.
- If you uninstall the app without deleting your account, your anonymous account remains, but nothing links it to you personally.

## 7. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The database uses row-level security, so each user can only access their own private data. Prayer records are never exposed to the app.

## 8. Children

Bil-Ghayb is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has posted on the app, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:
- **See** your needs and statistics in the app (Me tab).
- **Correct** your name, country and language in the app.
- **Delete** everything with one tap (Me → "Delete my account & data").
- **Contact us** for any other request (section 11).

Depending on where you live (for example, under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, the EU/UK GDPR, or California's CCPA), you may have additional rights. We will honour them. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
