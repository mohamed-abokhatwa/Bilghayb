Bil-Ghayb · بالغيب

Pray for a stranger. A stranger prays for you. ادعُ لغريب، ويدعو لك غريب.

Bil-Ghayb is a free app where strangers pray for each other anonymously. Each morning you receive one person and their need. You make dua for them, and somewhere, someone makes dua for you. No names, no chat, no money, no ads.

"No Muslim servant supplicates for his brother in his absence except that the angel says: 'And for you the same.'" (Sahih Muslim 2733)

Support

Questions, problems or a report about content? Write to mohamed.abokhatwa@hotmail.com. We reply as soon as we can, and act on content reports within 24 hours.

Common questions

  • Is it really free? Yes. There are no ads, no payments and no donations. Only dua.
  • Who can see my needs? Other users see your need, your first name only if you chose to share it, and your country. Nobody can ever see who prayed for whom.
  • How do I report or block someone? Tap the ⋯ on any need.
  • How do I delete my data? In the app: Me → "Delete my account & data". It is deleted immediately.
  • Which languages? Arabic, English, Urdu, Indonesian, Turkish, Bengali, French, Malay, Persian and German.
