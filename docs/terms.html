1. What Bil-Ghayb is

A free app where people pray (make dua) for each other anonymously. There are no payments, no ads and no chat.

2. Zero tolerance for abusive content

You must not post, in any need, name or note:

  • insults, hate, threats, harassment or bullying;
  • sexual or violent content;
  • mockery of Islam or of any faith;
  • requests to curse or harm anyone;
  • phone numbers, emails, usernames, links, messaging apps or any other contact details;
  • requests for money, donations or payment details;
  • personal information about other people;
  • spam, advertising or anything false or misleading.

Content that breaks these rules is removed, usually within 24 hours of a report. Accounts that break them are permanently removed.

3. Reporting and blocking

Every need has a ⋯ menu to report it or block its author. A need reported by three different people is hidden automatically until we review it. Blocking hides everything that person writes from you; they are not told.

4. Your content

You keep ownership of what you write. By posting, you allow us to show it to other users of the app for the purpose of dua. You can delete everything you posted at any time from Me → "Delete my account & data".

5. Not a substitute for help

Bil-Ghayb is a place for dua. It is not medical, psychological, legal or emergency advice. If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a helpline (see findahelpline.com).

6. Age

You must be at least 13 years old to use Bil-Ghayb.

7. No warranty

The app is provided free and "as is". We do our best to keep it available and safe, but we cannot guarantee it will always work without interruption.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. If the change is important, we will tell you in the app. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated terms.
