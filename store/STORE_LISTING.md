**Description:**

> "No Muslim servant supplicates for his brother in his absence except that the angel says: 'And for you the same.'" (Sahih Muslim 2733)

Bil-Ghayb turns this hadith into a daily habit.

EVERY MORNING, ONE PERSON
After Fajr you receive one stranger from somewhere in the world and what weighs on their heart: a mother's surgery, exams, debt, grief, a new Muslim whose family stopped speaking to them. You make dua for them, then hold the golden button.

SOMEONE PRAYS FOR YOU
Share your own need, by first name or anonymously. Strangers around the world will pray for you without ever knowing who you are. See how many people prayed for you this week, and from which countries.

HIDDEN, BY DESIGN
• No names exchanged, no chat, no followers
• Nobody can ever see who prayed for whom
• Phone numbers, contact details and money requests are blocked
• No email or phone number needed to sign up

MORE WAYS TO GIVE
• Pilgrim Mode: going for Umrah or Hajj? Carry the duas of those who cannot travel
• Janazah: pray for those who have returned to Allah
• Answered: read and share duas Allah has answered
• A suggested authentic dua for every need, with its reference
• Reminders after Fajr, in the last third of the night, and on Friday

FOR EVERYONE
Available in Arabic, English, Urdu, Indonesian, Turkish, Bengali, French, Malay, Persian and German. Needs in other languages show a Translate button.

FREE FOREVER
No ads. No payments. No donations. Only dua.

## Apple: App Privacy ("nutrition label")

In App Store Connect → App Privacy, answer:

- **Do you collect data?** Yes.
- **Tracking:** No. The app does not track users across apps or websites.

| Data type | Collected | Linked to user | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name (first name, optional) | Yes | Yes | App functionality |
| Other user content (needs, thank-you notes) | Yes | Yes | App functionality |
| User ID (anonymous account ID) | Yes | Yes | App functionality |
| Device ID (push notification token) | Yes | Yes | App functionality |
| Other data: country, language, time zone | Yes | Yes | App functionality |

Location: **not collected**. The country is picked by the user, never detected from the device.

Not collected: contact info, health, financial, precise location, contacts, browsing history, search history, purchases, diagnostics, usage data, advertising data.

## Google Play: Data safety

- **Does your app collect or share user data?** Collects: yes. Shares: no.
- **Is all data encrypted in transit?** Yes.
- **Can users request deletion?** Yes, in the app (Me → Delete my account & data), and by email.
- Data types collected (all required for app functionality, none shared, none for ads or analytics):
  - Personal info → Name (optional)
  - Messages → Other in-app messages (the needs people write)
  - App info and performance: none
  - Device or other IDs → yes (push token, anonymous account ID)

## Notes for the Apple reviewer (App Review Information → Notes)

> Bil-Ghayb is a free, anonymous dua (prayer) exchange. No login is required: an anonymous account is created automatically on first launch, so there are no demo credentials.
>
> How to test: choose a language, go through the 4 short intro screens, tap Begin. "Today" shows one stranger's need; press and hold the heart button to confirm a prayer. "Ask" lets you post a need. "Answered" shows answered prayers. "Me" has settings, language, and account deletion.
>
> User-generated content safeguards (Guideline 1.2): users agree to the Terms of Use (zero tolerance) before using the app; contact details, links and money requests are filtered automatically in 10 languages; every need has a ⋯ menu to report it or block its author; a need reported by 3 users is hidden automatically; reports are reviewed within 24 hours; the developer contact is in the app (Me → Support).
>
> Account deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v)): Me → "Delete my account & data" deletes everything immediately.
>
> The app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking.
